## What's new

### Videos

- **Save a video** of a view with the film button in the right bar. Choose the length, 24, 30 or 60 frames per second, and the size; every frame is antialiased like a view at rest. The video starts at time 0 and is an MP4 file (H.264), which plays everywhere.
- **Without a window:** `fractalstreamc --video zoom.mp4 --seconds 20 SCRIPT`, with `--from`, `--fps`, `--size`, `--viewer` and `--samples` (see `fractalstreamc --help`). One moment, as a picture: `--render picture.png --set .time=12 SCRIPT`.

### Views that move

- An `@prepare` that reads `.time` now runs at every frame, so it can set a view's center and radius from the time: zooms and pans, in the app and in videos. Views of the Riemann sphere follow their center and radius too.
- New example, **4 - More / 4 - Moving views**: a zoom on the Misiurewicz point c = i, where the Mandelbrot set looks more and more like the Julia set of i (Tan Lei's theorem).

### Faster

- Views that change (dragged, zoomed, animated) are drawn whole in every frame, with fewer pixels when the GPU can't keep up, and sharp again once they rest.
- Viewers no longer copy, at every pixel, the lists they only read: scripts with long lists draw much faster (2.6 times faster for the slow mating below).

### New example

- **5 - Gallery / 1 - Slow mating**: the filled Julia sets of z² + p and z² + q, glued along an equipotential. As its potential goes down to 0, they merge into their mating (Milnor's slow mating, drawn with Buff's method; Basilica and Rabbit by default).

### Fixes

- A function's result named as one of its parameters (`define orbit(z, c) = z where ...`) is now that parameter, so it starts from the argument; it used to start at 0, silently.
